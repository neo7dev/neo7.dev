## What this section is for

Docs here are reference: things that stay true and get looked up again. A page
earns a spot once its setup has been run start to finish and survived a reboot.

Anything time-bound — a debugging session, a decision and why it was made, a
thing that broke — belongs in the [blog](/blog) instead.

## How pages are written

Each page assumes you came for one answer and wants to get out of your way.
Commands are the ones that were actually run, with the paths left real enough to
be useful and nothing invented to fill a gap.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
Where a page has not been re-tested against a current release, it says so. An
untested page that looks confident is worse than no page.
{{< /callout >}}
